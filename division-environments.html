Private commissions · Art · Architecture · Landscape
The whole, not the parts — residences, collections and gardens conceived as one continuous work.

Environments is the house's most complete division: a single commission that integrates art, objects, architecture and landscape into one coherent whole.

The house acts as one point of responsibility — curating the collection, commissioning new works, and directing architects and landscape designers so that nothing in the finished environment feels placed; everything feels inevitable.

The environment as artwork

Nothing incidental. Nothing merely decorative.

Every material, object, artwork, view and transition is considered in relationship to the whole. The objective is not abundance. It is coherence.

How a commission moves

Every engagement follows the same quiet sequence, from first conversation to final placement.

01

Vision

A working session with the owners — how the place should feel, live and age.

02

Collection plan

A curated plan across editions, objects and commissioned works.

03

Design development

Architects, interior teams and landscape designers directed toward one integrated brief.

04

Fabrication & install

Works are made, positioned, documented and installed with attention to the final spatial experience.
